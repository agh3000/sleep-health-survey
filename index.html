Sleep Health Experiences Survey

We're learning how people seek answers about sleep, what they have tried, and where they run into difficulties. About 4–6 minutes.

Voluntary for adults 18+. Name, email and phone are all optional. Please do not include personal or sensitive medical information. This is customer research, not a clinical service.

Contact details (all optional)

Leave any or all fields blank to submit without sharing contact information.

Sharing contact details is not required and does not subscribe you to marketing.

1. Where are you based?

2. How often has sleep been a concern in the past six months?

3. What have you actually tried?

4. Did your last attempt give you useful answers?

5. What was the biggest obstacle?

6. Have you considered formal sleep testing?

7. If you considered a test but did not proceed, what stopped you?

8. How much have you spent on sleep-related services or products in the past year?

9. How satisfied are you with available sleep-health options?

10. What would be most useful to you?

11. What one thing would have made your experience easier?

Optional. Describe the process, not personal medical information.

Answers are processed by FormSubmit and emailed to the survey organizer. Email recipient activation may be required.
